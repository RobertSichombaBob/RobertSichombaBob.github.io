About Me

A geoscientist who speaks data.

I'm an Exploration Geologist and Data Scientist based in North Western Zambia, with over 3 years of combined experience across mineral exploration, geophysics, geotechnical engineering and road construction materials.

My work sits at the intersection of the field and the database. I've managed the full exploration pipeline — soil and stream sediment sampling, diamond drilling supervision, core logging, resource estimation and ore control — while building the Python, SQL and PostGIS systems that make that data usable and auditable.

On the geotechnical side, I led a 10+ technician laboratory team on the Lusaka–Ndola Road Project, delivering 98% specification compliance and automating QA/QC reporting to cut reporting time by 40%.

I'm currently completing an MSc in Data Science and Exploration Geology (a Kobold Metals sponsored programme) — deepening my work in geostatistics, machine learning and mineral prospectivity mapping.

I take on consulting and contract work in exploration targeting, geophysical interpretation, geochemical data analysis, GIS, database design and materials QA/QC.

Services

What I can do for your project

Six service areas spanning the full geoscience workflow — from greenfield targeting to compliant laboratory QA/QC. Engaged per project, retainer or day rate.

Mineral Exploration & Drill Targeting

End-to-end exploration programme design and execution, from target generation to drill-ready prospects.

  • Soil, stream sediment & rock chip sampling programmes
  • Diamond drilling supervision & core logging
  • Structural interpretation & 3D geological modelling
  • Resource estimation & ore control support

Geophysics: Acquisition & Interpretation

Field acquisition and 2D/3D interpretation of geophysical datasets for lithological and structural mapping.

  • Ground magnetics & Induced Polarization (IP)
  • Magnetic susceptibility & gamma spectrometry
  • Portable XRF (pXRF) characterisation
  • Downhole logging & anomaly delineation

Geotechnical & Materials Engineering

Site investigation, materials characterisation and laboratory QA/QC for roads, civil works and mining infrastructure.

  • Borehole logging, SPT & in-situ testing
  • Borrow pit exploration & reserve assessment
  • Soils, aggregates, asphalt & concrete testing
  • Compaction control & specification compliance

Geochemical Data Science & Statistics

Turn raw assay and geochemical datasets into ranked anomalies and defensible drill targets.

  • Compositional Data Analysis (CODA) & PCA
  • Multivariate statistics, clustering & regression
  • Machine learning for prospectivity mapping
  • Variography, kriging & spatial interpolation

GIS & 3D Spatial Modelling

Complete QGIS/ArcGIS project setup, map production and spatial analysis for technical reporting.

  • End-to-end QGIS project configuration
  • Georeferencing, digitising & map packages
  • Interpolated geochemical & geophysical grids
  • Cross-sections & 3D visualisation

Exploration Databases & QA/QC Systems

Build the data infrastructure that keeps exploration and laboratory programmes audit-ready.

  • PostgreSQL/PostGIS database design
  • Automated validation, CRMs, blanks & duplicates
  • Python QA/QC reporting pipelines
  • Interactive dashboards & live monitoring
